ROUTE 88: ZOMBIE OUTBREAK

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026.

This policy covers the current browser playtest and the planned iOS release. The browser playtest has no AdMob ads or Apple purchases. Native iOS advertising and purchases are still being tested and are not available in the public playtest.

Scott Natoli develops Route 88: Zombie Outbreak. This policy explains information handled by the browser playtest and the planned iOS game. The advertising, age-choice, and Apple purchase features described below apply to the planned native iOS release when enabled. For support or a privacy request, email chaos13.sn@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

The browser playtest keeps campaign progress, currency, upgrades, and settings on your device. The planned iOS version also keeps an offline Remove Ads access cache saved after verified Apple ownership. It does not require a separate game account or provide a developer-operated cloud-save service. Our game code does not request your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location.

Advertising

The planned free iOS version will use Google AdMob for ads between selected completed levels and optional reward offers. Ads are not shown during combat. Optional rewards require you to choose the offer and complete the rewarded ad. Buying Remove Ads suppresses advertising.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process network addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, app or device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent abuse, and operate their services. Restricted or non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

The game requests privacy choices through Google's User Messaging Platform where required and checks whether ads may be requested. Its publisher settings request non-personalized ads and disable the publisher first-party ID. Our game code does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, and we do not enable AdMob's optional IDFA explainer message. These choices do not mean advertising services collect no information.

Age and privacy choices

Route 88 is intended for adults and teens and is not directed at children under 13. The advertising screen asks only whether you are 18 or older, 13–17, or prefer not to say; it does not ask for your birth date. The choice is used for the current session. Teen players receive restricted advertising treatment. Choosing not to share an age keeps ads off: the game does not start Google's advertising service or request new ads after that choice. Advertising libraries can still load and prepare app and device information when the game starts, and an already-started service cannot be fully unloaded by this setting. Google may receive restriction flags needed to apply these choices.

You can revisit advertising age choices and available Google privacy options in the game's settings. Changing a privacy choice invalidates cached ads before further requests. You may also use the privacy settings available on your device.

Remove Ads purchases

In the planned iOS release, Apple will process the optional, one-time Remove Ads purchase. The game receives product information, a localized price, transaction information, and verified entitlement results to deliver and restore the purchase. We do not receive your payment-card details. Store information may be requested when the game checks existing ownership, even if you do not buy anything.

Unity In-App Purchasing is also used to connect the game to Apple's store. Its services may process installation, device, session, country, purchase, and vendor-identifier information for commerce, service operation, reporting, and security. Disabling optional Unity advertising or analytics consent does not establish that purchase services send no data. Apple and Unity maintain their own service records.

Retention and your requests

Local saves and settings remain until you reset them or remove the app, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior. Restore Purchases can recover a valid Apple entitlement after reinstalling or changing devices. Removing local data does not remove Apple's or an advertising provider's records.

For questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information handled on our behalf, contact us at the email above. Tell us which service or transaction is involved; do not send passwords or payment-card details. Applicable rights and required record retention depend on your location and the service involved. Provider privacy pages explain their processing, international transfers, retention, and request procedures.
