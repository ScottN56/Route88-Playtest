Scott Natoli develops Route 88: Zombie Outbreak. This policy explains information handled by the public browser playtest, private internal iOS TestFlight build, and planned iOS game. Advertising and age-choice features described below apply to the native iOS game when enabled. The private TestFlight build is configured to use Apple purchase services, subject to store availability. For support or a privacy request, email chaos13.sn@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

The browser playtest and private internal iOS TestFlight build keep campaign progress, currency, upgrades, and settings on your device. The iOS purchase integration can also keep an offline Remove Ads access cache after verified Apple ownership. It does not require a separate game account or provide a developer-operated cloud-save service. Our game code does not request your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location.

Advertising

The planned free iOS version will use Google AdMob for ads between selected completed levels and optional reward offers. Ads are not shown during combat. For players without Remove Ads, optional ad-supported rewards require choosing the offer and completing the rewarded ad. Verified Remove Ads ownership suppresses all ads and lets players claim earned, eligible objective bonuses without watching ads.

The separate internal test candidate uses Route 88's publisher app identifier to select Google privacy messages and Google-provided test ad units for rewarded and level-end ads. These units display test creatives instead of production ads. They are not offline simulations: privacy-message updates, ad requests, and SDK initialization may contact Google even during testing. This policy's provider and data-processing qualifications also apply to those test services.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process network addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, app or device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent abuse, and operate their services. Restricted or non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

The game requests privacy choices through Google's User Messaging Platform where required and checks whether ads may be requested. Its publisher settings request non-personalized ads and disable the publisher first-party ID. Our game code does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, and we do not enable AdMob's optional IDFA explainer message. These choices do not mean advertising services collect no information.

Age and privacy choices

Route 88 is intended for adults and teens and is not directed at children under 13. The advertising screen asks only whether you are 18 or older, 13–17, or prefer not to say; it does not ask for your birth date. The choice is used for the current session. Teen players receive restricted advertising treatment. Choosing not to share an age keeps ads off: the game does not start Google's advertising service or request new ads after that choice. Advertising libraries can still load and prepare app and device information when the game starts, and an already-started service cannot be fully unloaded by this setting. Google may receive restriction flags needed to apply these choices.

You can revisit advertising age choices and available Google privacy options in the game's settings. Changing a privacy choice invalidates cached ads before further requests. You may also use the privacy settings available on your device.

Remove Ads purchases

The installed internal iOS build 1 and the separate build 2 test candidate have Apple purchase integration configured, although product pricing, native purchases, and restoration have not yet been verified. When the store is available, Apple handles the optional, one-time Remove Ads purchase. The integration may request product information, a localized price, transaction information, and verified entitlement results to deliver and restore a purchase. Store metadata and existing ownership may be checked even if you do not buy anything. We do not receive your payment-card details. The newer test candidate includes a RETRY PRICE option when a safe retry is available. This refreshes product information on the existing store connection and may refresh ownership; it does not start a purchase. Requests that have not safely finished require reopening the game. These statements describe the configured integration, not observed network traffic.

Unity In-App Purchasing is also used to connect the game to Apple's store. Its services may process installation, device, session, country, purchase, and vendor-identifier information for commerce, service operation, reporting, and security. Disabling optional Unity advertising or analytics consent does not establish that purchase services send no data. Apple and Unity maintain their own service records.

Retention and your requests

Local saves and settings remain until you reset them or remove the app, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior. Restore Purchases can recover a valid Apple entitlement after reinstalling or changing devices. Removing local data does not remove Apple's or an advertising provider's records.

Tell us which service or transaction is involved; do not send passwords or payment-card details. Applicable rights and required record retention depend on your location and the service involved.
