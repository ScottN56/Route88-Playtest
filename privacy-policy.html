Privacy Policy

It uses Google-provided test ads and Apple sandbox purchases. The developer has verified test-ad playback, price availability, Remove Ads purchase, ad-free relaunch, and purchase restoration on an iPhone. Regional consent and other purchase outcomes remain under review.

Scott Natoli develops Route 88: Zombie Outbreak. This policy explains information handled by the public browser playtest, private internal iOS TestFlight build, and planned iOS game. Advertising and age-choice features described below apply to the native iOS game when enabled. The private TestFlight build is configured to use Apple purchase services, subject to store availability. For support or a privacy request, email chaos13.sn@gmail.com.

Game progress and settings

The browser playtest and private internal iOS TestFlight build keep campaign progress, currency, upgrades, and settings on your device. The iOS purchase integration can also keep an offline Remove Ads access cache after verified Apple ownership. It does not require a separate game account or provide a developer-operated cloud-save service. Our game code does not request your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location.

Advertising

The planned free iOS version will use Google AdMob for ads between selected completed levels and optional reward offers. Ads are not shown during combat. For players without Remove Ads, optional ad-supported rewards require choosing the offer and completing the rewarded ad. Verified Remove Ads ownership suppresses all ads and lets players claim earned, eligible objective bonuses without watching ads.

The current internal iOS TestFlight build uses Route 88's publisher app identifier to select Google privacy messages and Google-provided test ad units for rewarded and level-end ads. These units display test creatives instead of production ads. They are not offline simulations: privacy-message updates, ad requests, and SDK initialization may contact Google even during testing. This policy's provider and data-processing qualifications also apply to those test services.

When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process network addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, app or device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent abuse, and operate their services. Restricted or non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

The game requests privacy choices through Google's User Messaging Platform where required and checks whether ads may be requested. Its publisher settings request non-personalized ads and disable the publisher first-party ID. Our game code does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, and we do not enable AdMob's optional IDFA explainer message. These choices do not mean advertising services collect no information.

Age and privacy choices

Route 88 contains frequent zombie combat, gun use, blood effects, horror, and a story about family separation and trauma. Apple's current calculated App Store age rating is 18+ in most regions, with regional and older-operating-system differences. It is not directed at children under 13. The advertising screen asks only whether you are 18 or older, 13–17, or prefer not to say; it does not ask for your birth date. This choice controls advertising restrictions, not access to the game's content. Teen players and players who prefer not to disclose an age receive restricted advertising treatment whenever Google's privacy rules permit ads. Choosing not to share an age does not unlock Remove Ads or permanently turn ads off. Required consent and device privacy choices can still prevent an ad request. Google may receive restriction flags needed to apply these choices.

You can revisit advertising age choices and available Google privacy options in the game's settings. Changing a privacy choice invalidates cached ads before further requests. You may also use the privacy settings available on your device.

Remove Ads purchases

Apple handles the optional, one-time Remove Ads purchase when the store is available. Its planned U.S. price is $1.00; Apple's localized price is shown before a purchase. The current internal iOS TestFlight build uses Apple's sandbox, which is separate from a customer purchase. The integration requests product information, localized prices, transaction information, and verified entitlement results to deliver and restore purchases. Store metadata and existing ownership may be checked even if you do not buy anything. We do not receive your payment-card details. RETRY PRICE refreshes product information on the existing store connection and may refresh ownership; it does not start a purchase. Successful price loading, purchase, ad-free relaunch, and restoration have been checked on an iPhone in build 3; cancelled, deferred, refunded, and revoked purchases remain to be verified.

Unity In-App Purchasing 5.4 is used to connect the game to Apple's store. Its services can process installation, device, session, country, purchase, and vendor-identifier information for commerce, service operation, reporting, and security. In the installed package, Apple vendor identifiers and purchase-event information can be prepared even when optional Unity advertising and analytics consent is denied. Our game does not enable Unity advertising or optional Unity Analytics, link a third-party analytics service, or use Unity web payments. These settings do not establish that purchase services send no data. Apple and Unity maintain their own service records. Requests about Unity IAP records processed on our behalf can be sent to the support address below, so we can route a verified request to Unity.

Retention and your requests

Local saves and settings remain until you reset them or remove the app, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior. Restore Purchases can recover a valid Apple entitlement after reinstalling or changing devices. Removing local data does not remove Apple's or an advertising provider's records.

For questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information handled on our behalf, contact us at the email above. Tell us which service or transaction is involved; do not send passwords or payment-card details. Applicable rights and required record retention depend on your location and the service involved.
