Movement, aiming, and Dash

Use the left stick to move. Use the right stick to aim and fire, and tap DASH above it to evade. You can move and aim at the same time. Open Pause to adjust sound and other settings.

If sound does not start

Open the playtest in Safari, tap the start prompt, then enter the game with sound. Check your phone volume and the game's music and effects settings.

Saved progress

Progress is saved on your device. Use the same browser and device to continue the browser playtest. Clearing website data or using private browsing can remove your save. The game does not currently provide a developer-operated cloud save.

Reporting a problem

Tell us whether the issue occurs in the browser playtest or a native test build. Include the level or screen name and steps to reproduce it. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or signing keys.

Advertising and purchases

The planned iOS release will be free with ads and an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase. Store pricing, purchases, restoration, and advertising are still being tested. They are not active in the public browser playtest.

Internal iOS test candidate

If the new build 2 candidate is made available to you, its rewarded and level-end ads use Google test creatives. It uses Route 88's publisher configuration for Google privacy messages, so it may contact Google while checking privacy choices or loading test ads. Test ads do not mean no data is processed. Age and privacy choices, verified Remove Ads ownership, and safe gameplay moments still control ad eligibility. In Settings, use AD AGE OPTIONS; AD PRIVACY OPTIONS is available when Google requires it. No birth date is requested.

In that newer candidate, open Remove Ads and use RETRY PRICE when available. It refreshes store information without starting a purchase. If an unfinished request blocks retry, close and reopen the game. Existing verified ad-free access is preserved during price failures. Price availability, purchases, restoration, consent messages, and ad playback still require testing on the phone.

When reporting a native test issue, include the version and build number shown in TestFlight, the screen, and whether it happened before or after an age or privacy choice. Do not send receipts, transaction identifiers, or private account documents.
